About HireLens AI

HireLens AI is an explainable resume-screening application designed to help compare multiple candidates against job requirements quickly and clearly.

🎯

Our Purpose

Help reduce the time required to manually compare resumes by organizing important candidate information.

📄

Resume Screening

Multiple PDF resumes can be screened against a single job description in one process.

📊

Explainable Scoring

Candidate scores are calculated using weighted skill matching so the result can be understood and reviewed.

🔍

Transparent Results

Results show matched skills, missing skills, strengths, skill gaps, experience overview, and recommendation.

🏆

Candidate Ranking

Candidates are ranked by their match score and grouped into shortlist, review, or not shortlisted status.

🚀

Future Improvements

Future versions could include semantic skill matching, improved experience analysis, and more configurable recruiter criteria.
