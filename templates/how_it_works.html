How HireLens AI Works

HireLens AI compares uploaded resumes with the skills mentioned in a job description and produces an explainable candidate ranking.

1

Enter Job Requirements

Add the job description containing the skills and requirements you are looking for.

2

Upload Resumes

Upload one or multiple PDF resumes for screening.

3

Detect Relevant Skills

HireLens detects supported skills from both the job description and each resume.

4

Calculate Match Score

Required skills are compared using weighted skill matching to calculate a candidate match score.

5

Rank Candidates

Candidates are automatically sorted from the highest match score to the lowest.

6

Review the Explanation

View matched skills, missing skills, strengths, skill gaps, experience overview, and shortlist status.
